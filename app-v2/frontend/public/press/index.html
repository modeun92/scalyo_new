About Scalyo

Boilerplate description, to be used as is.

Scalyo is a B2B Customer Success platform that helps CS teams run both their accounts and their team: client portfolio and health scores, KPIs, retention playbooks, planning and tasks, AI agents, and wellbeing tracking for Customer Success Managers. The platform is natively multilingual — French, English and Korean.

Scalyo was founded by Lidia Chikhoune, a former Customer Success Manager, on one conviction: a burnt-out CSM saves no account. Where other tools measure how accounts perform, Scalyo also measures the load carried by the people looking after them.
